# Add a new quiche recipe feature that provides users with complete ingredients, step-by-step preparation instructions, and baking specifications.

## Requirements

- The system shall display a new quiche recipe upon user request.
- The recipe shall include a comprehensive list of ingredients with precise measurements.
- The recipe shall provide sequential, step-by-step instructions for crust preparation, filling assembly, and baking.
- The recipe shall specify recommended oven temperature, baking duration, and resting time.
- The recipe content shall be structured for clear readability and easy reference during preparation.
